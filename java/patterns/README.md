# Problem Solving Approaches

## 1. Backtracking
- **Identification:** Recursive exploration of all possibilities with decisions made & undone at each step.
- **Input:** Decision space or a set of choices.
- **Constraints:** Conditions that guide the exploration.
- **Output:** Solution or set of solutions.
- **Wordings:**
  - "Find all possible combinations..."
  - "Generate all valid solutions..."
- **Requirements:** Depth-first search (DFS) is often suitable.

## 2. Bit Manipulation
- **Identification:** Operations on binary representations & efficient use of bitwise operators.
- **Input:** Integer values or binary.
- **Constraints:** Limited to bit-level operations.
- **Output:** Modified binary or specific bits set/reset.
- **Wordings:**
  - "Optimize space using bitwise operations..."
  - "Perform operations at the bit level..."
- **Requirements:** Familiarity with bitwise operators.

## 3. Intervals
- **Identification:** Problems with ranges or intervals.
- **Input:** List of intervals or ranges.
- **Constraints:** Conditions on overlapping or merging intervals.
- **Output:** Modified or merged intervals.
- **Wordings:**
  - "Merge overlapping intervals..."
  - "Find the minimum number of intervals to cover..."
- **Requirements:** Sorting and merging techniques.

## 4. Marking
- **Identification:** Use flags or markers during iteration.
- **Input:** Collection of elements.
- **Constraints:** Conditions for marking or unmarking elements.
- **Output:** Modified collection based on marking.
- **Wordings:**
  - "Mark elements meeting certain conditions..."
  - "Identify & process marked elements..."
  - "Negate the values of elements meeting certain criteria..."
  - "Invert the binary representation of an integer."
- **Requirements:** Efficient marking/unmarking techniques.

## 5. PrefixSuffix
- **Identification:** Process prefixes or suffixes of a sequence.
- **Input:** Sequence or array.
- **Constraints:** Conditions related to prefixes/suffixes.
- **Output:** Result based on prefixes/suffixes.
- **Wordings:**
  - "Find the longest common prefix..."
  - "Compute the sum of all subarrays..."
  - "Find the largest product of contiguous subarrays..."
  - "Compute the sum of elements in a specific range of an array."
  - "Calculate the product of elements except for the current element."
- **Requirements:** Prefix or suffix array computation.

## 6. Recursion
- **Identification:** Problems naturally solved by calling the same function.
- **Input:** Problem space that can be broken down into subproblems.
- **Constraints:** Recursive conditions.
- **Output:** Solution to the entire problem.
- **Wordings:**
  - "Divide and conquer..."
  - "Solve smaller instances of the same problem..."
- **Requirements:** Well-defined base case.

## 7. Sliding Window
- **Identification:** Sequentially process elements in a "window."
- **Input:** Sequential data or array.
- **Constraints:** Fixed-size window, conditions for moving it.
- **Output:** Result based on the window.
- **Wordings:**
  - "Find the maximum sum *subarray* of length k..."
  - "Longest *substring* with at most two distinct characters..."
  - "Find the first negative number in every *subarray* of size K."
  - "Identify the longest *subarray* with at most K distinct elements."
  - "Determine the shortest *subarray* with a sum exceeding a given threshold."
- **Requirements:** Efficient window movement and tracking.

## 8. Two Pointers
- **Identification:** Maintain two pointers, often for iteration.
- **Input:** Sequential data or array.
- **Constraints:** Conditions for moving pointers.
- **Output:** Result based on pointer positions.
- **Wordings:**
  - "Find a pair with a given sum in the array..."
  - "Remove duplicates from a sorted array..."
  - "Rearrange a sorted array in non-decreasing order..."
  - "Find closest triplet sum to target in array."
  - "Check for triplet with target sum in sorted array."
- **Requirements:** Efficient pointer movement and conditions.
